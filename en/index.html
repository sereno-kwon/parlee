Comfort is performance.
In pursuit of the finest
carbon fiber bicycle in the world.

Parlee Cycles is a carbon fiber bicycle brand founded in 2000 by Bob Parlee in Beverly, Massachusetts. After more than twenty years working with composites as a boat builder, he started Parlee with a single goal: to build the best bicycle in the world. Every Parlee is built one at a time by the hands of skilled craftspeople, guided by Bob Parlee's design philosophy.

Parlee sees performance through the lens of 'energy management'. Whether a ride lasts 40 minutes, 4 hours or 4 days, the less energy you lose to road vibration and fatigue, the more power you have left. Sophisticated carbon layups, fit components tailored to the rider's body, and geometry that fits more riders all grow from this philosophy.

FOUNDER · BOB PARLEE

“We founded Parlee in 2000 with one simple but ambitious goal: to build the best bicycle in the world.”

After more than twenty years in boat building, I knew what composites were capable of. Carbon fiber was clearly the material that could take performance bicycles to the next level, but at the time its potential was far from fully realized. Carbon lets you optimize the size and shape of each tube and finely tune weight, strength, durability and compliance. The more you refine layup schedules and molding techniques, the lighter, faster and more comfortable the bike becomes.

Today carbon fiber is the material of nearly every performance bicycle, and with that, carbon can turn designers into stylists. But style must never be mistaken for performance. Unnecessary shapes only add weight and degrade the ride. It is like putting fins on a car: it may look great, but it will not make the car any faster.

Our principle is efficiency. From road racing and daily training to cyclocross and all-day gravel adventures, we use carbon strategically to deliver exactly the performance each purpose demands. Performance tuned to the goal and fit tuned to the body: that is what defines a Parlee.

Bob Parlee, founder of Parlee Cycles

IN-DEPTH

Vive la vida Ouray

The Ouray is Parlee's vision of the modern road bike. Quicker off the line, more stable at speed, and designed for real-world road conditions. Where other road platforms emphasize either comfort or speed, the Ouray strikes a balance between the two, creating a bike that is fast, confident and eager to go any distance.

Its light construction and refined handling make it feel natural on long solo rides, segment hunts and weekend adventures alike. A bike that lets you hold race-bike speed all day long: that is the Ouray.

A NEW ERA

Lightweight,
but more than just a lightweight.

Building on the legacy of the Z5 and the Altum, the Ouray opens a new era for Parlee road bikes. A sloping top tube and dropped stays tune compliance for all-day comfort, while improved lateral and torsional stiffness carries speed without effort. A stable wheelbase keeps the bike planted on the road, and the monocoque carbon frame filters out road buzz without dulling the feedback from the surface.

Aerodynamically clean lines and purpose-driven shapes cut drag, preserving momentum on long, exposed climbs and descents. The result is clear: a road bike that is decisively faster, lighter and more capable than the generation before it.

PURPOSE BUILT

Unshakable composure

Where the Ouray truly shines is its composure. Into a corner, it grips the asphalt with a planted confidence that keeps you calm and fast. Broken pavement, endless roadworks and neglected country lanes pass beneath it without hesitation.

With clearance for tires up to 40mm, you can run wider rubber for comfort, grip or winter roads without losing speed. Enjoy race-bike speed without the aches, and without white-knuckling the bars.

A PERFECT FINISH

In the Nude: carbon with nothing to hide

Designed by Parlee's engineering team and finished by hand by craftspeople in Portugal, the Ouray frame is built with a precise carbon layup that leaves no excess weight. Because every ply sits exactly where it should, no filler, cosmetic layers or adhesives are needed. So there is nothing to hide.

This method demands the highest level of carbon expertise, which is why few other manufacturers attempt it. Thanks to it, each frame emerges from the mold with a flawless surface. That is why Parlee offers the Ouray in raw carbon with a subtle wax coating.

A LEGACY OF FIT

Styled for You

Every frame is made in small batches, one at a time, and individually assembled to order at Parlee's headquarters in the US. Once you choose a frame size, fit components are selected to match your body measurements, so the bike suits both your size and your riding style.

With Parlee Paint Lab, the customization possibilities are (nearly) endless. Thanks to size-specific geometry and personalized component choices, the Ouray fits more riders well, right from the start.

ALL-NEW CONCEPT

The Future is Now

With longer, slacker geometry, the Taos makes a bold break from conventional gravel bikes. But every choice has a reason. One look tells you this is no road bike with wide tires squeezed in. The Taos is a concept designed from the ground up to inspire almost addictive confidence.

The monocoque frame construction is not only extremely light but also delivers better power transfer. A torsionally stiff lower section and a compact, sloping front triangle smooth out the ride and make the rider faster.

CHALLENGE ACCEPTED

Intentional, and exceptional

Progressive design and a meticulously refined carbon layup deliver an entirely new gravel experience. A long reach and slack head angle provide confident, stable handling at speed, while a low BB and short chainstays smooth out rough surfaces and keep the bike planted through corners and descents.

With high-speed stability and maximum power transfer, it conquers rough terrain, devours the flats and flies up the climbs. Each of the six frame sizes has its own dedicated layup and geometry, delivering the same ride from the smallest size to the largest.

JACK OF ALL TERRAINS

Asphalt? Singletrack?
With the Taos, yes.

With generous 50mm tire clearance and suspension-corrected geometry, the off-road options are endless. A removable front derailleur mount supports both 1x and 2x setups, and you can add a suspension fork or even choose mountain bike tires without compromising the ride.

Simply swapping tires delivers an outstanding ride on completely different surfaces. Smooth, low-tread tires suit the pavement, while wide knobby tires take on rougher terrain. Generous internal down tube storage, fender mounts, top tube bag mounts and stealth dropper post routing. Whether you are racing the clock or chasing the sun, it is ready for any adventure.

PERFECTLY NAKED

Lay it Bare

The precision layup, made by hand by craftspeople in Portugal, carries no dead weight. Every carbon ply is placed exactly where intended, creating a frame that needs no cosmetic layers, filler or adhesives. Unlike mass-produced carbon frames, there is nothing to cover up.

This painstaking construction produces a perfect surface straight from the mold. That is why the Taos is offered in fully nude carbon with a wax finish. No need to worry about cosmetic damage like paint chips or scratches.

FROM PARTS TO PAINT

Made for You

Every frame is made in small batches, one at a time, and individually assembled to order at Parlee's headquarters in the US. Once you choose a frame size, fit components are selected to match your body measurements. Choose a standard build and paint, or pick every detail yourself. After all, it is your bike.

Logo color options, standard paint schemes, all the way to fully custom paint. The customization possibilities are (nearly) endless.

Z-ZERO GTBESPOKE · HAND-BUILT IN BEVERLY, MA

Exceptional Performance.
Epic Days. Big (S)miles.
The best just got better.

For more than 25 years, Parlee's Z Series has set the standard for craftsmanship, quality and iteratively refined design. Parlee's multi-award-winning flagship road platform is built by hand by craftspeople in a small workshop 45 minutes north of Boston.

Completely reimagined for 2025, the Z-Zero GT is a love letter to the road and a reaffirmation of the value of building a bike for one rider rather than for everyone. It is designed to cover long distances over countless mountains with speed and luxurious comfort. Wherever it goes, it is a modern classic that will turn heads for generations.

More than a bicycle

A Parlee is the culmination of engineering. It is a reaction against uniform design and anonymous mass production, and a testament to the value of bespoke craftsmanship. From the visible surfaces to the places no one sees, it pursues perfection to the extreme.

Not for everyone. For one.

Every custom Z Series reflects the rider's own style, pushing limits and outpacing the competition. The GT embodies the future of road cycling and an endless desire to explore.

Lightness, made lighter

The Z Series has always represented the highest standard of build quality. With the GT, the carbon layup has been optimized once again for comfort, responsiveness and astonishing speed. It is the fastest Z Series ever and the lightest bike in its family.

A new kind of smooth

A precise carbon layup refined over 25 years absorbs vibration, leaving only fine sensation and a pure connection to the road.

BETTER, FASTER, SMOOTHER

A Precise Powerhouse

The GT's handmade frame showcases the pinnacle of carbon fiber technology and delivers the smoothest ride in the world. Using Parlee's own methods, the carbon layup has been optimized even further, elevating comfort, responsiveness and astonishing speed together.

FUTURE-PROOF

Leading From the Front

An all-new layup and design shave a remarkable 187g off the previous model. A stronger, lighter full-carbon UDH chainstay module opens up compatibility with current and future drivetrains. The fully integrated fork, designed and built in-house by Parlee, offers a clean look with no exposed cables, one-piece bar and stem compatibility, and clearance for tires up to 40mm. Working in harmony, the frame and fork improve ride quality, aerodynamics and perceived speed alike.

COMFORT POWERS PERFORMANCE

Perfecting the Ride

The GT was redesigned from the ground up to maximize handling and boost rider comfort and efficiency. The BB sits 10mm lower than on a typical road bike, lowering the center of gravity, and this small, subtle change noticeably increases stability. The new, stiffer chainstay module transfers pedaling torque to the road without loss.

From asphalt to farm roads and the occasional gravel, the GT meets any surface with its signature confidence.

CUSTOM-MADE SPEED

A Bike for One, Not All

Each of the GT's 12 standard sizes (Race or Sport geometry) has its own reference geometry and ships with a combination of crank length, stem length, handlebar width, seatpost setback and saddle width tailored to the individual rider.

Reference sizes are only the beginning. Choose a fully bespoke Z-Zero GT and you get geometry and a frame configuration tailored to your profile. It is an ultra-precise process that makes the GT feel like part of your body. And Parlee Paint Lab turns any color and design you can imagine into reality.

25 YEARS OF LEARNING

What does it mean to build the best? In 1999, Bob Parlee set aside his career as a boat builder to answer that question. From day one, his goal was the best bicycle in the world, and every morning he woke up thinking about what the best could be.

A MASTERPIECE

BESPOKEYOU DREAM IT, WE BUILD IT

Custom doesn't have to be complicated.
12 reference geometries,
endless fit possibilities.

For more than 25 years, Parlee's mission has never changed: to build the best bicycle in the world. Every Parlee Z Series has always been made 100% by hand in Beverly, Massachusetts, for the one person who will ride it.

Parlee's unmatched customization capability is the result of a multilayered process that begins with one simple principle: listening. Parlee knows that the most important part of building a custom bike is communication between the rider, the fitter and the builder.

12 STARTING POINTS

By analyzing custom fit data gathered over more than 25 years, Parlee created 12 reference geometries. Each can be adjusted as far as the rider's body measurements and riding style require, resulting in the widest range of geometry available in custom carbon fiber.

WE SWEAT THE DETAILS

Every custom design is developed with Parlee's proprietary CAD system, which models the rider's contact points and aligns them with the actual frame geometry. Once the rider approves, the production team determines the exact dimensions and carbon layup schedule for a perfect fit and a better ride.

FIT SPECIFIC COMPONENTS

Every Parlee ships with components matched to the rider's measurements. Crank length, cockpit dimensions, saddle width and seatpost offset are chosen for each individual. Shimano, Campagnolo or SRAM groupsets, along with ZIPP, HED, ENVE or whatever else you want, can all be specified.

SHIPPED IN 90 DAYS

We aim for some of the fastest lead times in the custom industry. Schedules stay predictable, and riders can follow along as their bike is built. (Based on shipping from the US workshop.)
